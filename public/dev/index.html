PROJECT DUET

A shared wall for two.

WHAT THIS IS

What it is

A small private website for two people. Each of you has a wall. You stick notes and drawings on the other person's wall, and they're there the next time they open it. There are no feeds, likes, followers or ads.

Who it's for

Two people who want a calm place to leave things for each other: partners, friends, family. It isn't for groups, and it isn't a chat app. There are no accounts and no emails. You both use one passphrase.

How to start

  1. Choose your names and an animal each.
  2. Pick an address, like purrpause.space/mira-jo.
  3. Set a passphrase. Send the address and passphrase to the other person yourself.
  4. Send the request. Every new wall is approved by hand, so it can take a while, and some requests may be declined. Your request page updates itself once it's approved.
  5. Open your address, enter the passphrase, choose which of you you are, and start sticking notes.

Please know

  • A lost passphrase can't be recovered. Keep it somewhere safe.
  • Anyone with the address and passphrase can read and write on the wall.
  • I store your names, animals, address, a scrambled passphrase, a scrambled form of your network address (to limit abuse), and your notes.
  • This is a hobby project on free hosting. It can be slow or go down, with no guarantee. You can download your notes any time from Settings.

On trust. I run this site, so I can technically read stored notes. I'm working on a way to change that, so that not even I could. Until it's ready, it rests on trust: I won't read them.
