PROJECT DUET

A shared wall for two

WHAT THIS IS

A small private website for two people. Each of you has a wall. You stick notes and drawings on the other person's wall, and they are there the next time they open it.

No feeds, likes or ads. Only streaks, which you both keep going.

It is for two people who want a calm place to leave things for each other: partners, friends, family. It is not a chat app, and there are no accounts or emails.

  1. Pick your names and an animal each.
  2. Choose an address, like purrpause.space/coffee.
  3. Set a passphrase. Share the address and passphrase with the other person.
  4. Send the request. I approve every new wall myself, so it can take a while. Some requests are declined.
  5. Once approved, open your address, enter the passphrase and start sticking notes.
  • A lost passphrase can't be recovered. Keep it somewhere safe.
  • Anyone with the address and passphrase can read and write on the wall.
  • I store your names, animals, address, a scrambled passphrase, a scrambled form of your network address, and your notes.
  • I run this site, so I could technically read notes. I won't, and I'm working on a way so that I couldn't.
  • It's a hobby project on free hosting, so it can be slow or go down. You can download your notes from Settings any time.
